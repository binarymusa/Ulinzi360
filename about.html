Ulinzi 360 Consultancy and Security Management Limited is a Kenya-based security consultancy focused on risk management, intelligence analysis, governance, preparedness, and strategic security advisory.

OUR STORY

Building a more informed approach to security.

Ulinzi 360 was established in 2024 to address a growing need for structured, advisory-led security and risk management across institutions and organizations in Kenya.

The firm was founded on the principle that modern security challenges require more than reactive responses. Effective security requires intelligence, structured risk analysis, governance frameworks, strategic planning, and institutional preparedness.

Our approach brings these disciplines together to help organizations understand their security environment, identify vulnerabilities, make informed decisions, and prepare for emerging risks.

Ulinzi 360 operates with a vendor-neutral advisory philosophy, allowing security recommendations to remain focused on organizational requirements, risk exposure, and long-term resilience.

01

Vendor Neutrality

Our advisory approach is independent and focused on the organization's security requirements, risk environment, and operational priorities rather than promoting a particular product or provider.

02

Intelligence-Led

We place structured intelligence and analysis at the centre of security decision-making, helping organizations understand threats, vulnerabilities, and changing operating environments.

03

Integrity

We operate with professional accountability, confidentiality, disciplined processes, and respect for the sensitive nature of security information and organizational decisions.

04

Resilience Focus

Our objective extends beyond immediate response. We help organizations strengthen preparedness, governance, continuity, and their ability to respond effectively to changing risks.
