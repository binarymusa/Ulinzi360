METHODOLOGY

How We Work

Ulinzi 360 applies a structured advisory framework built around risk intelligence, contextual analysis, governance, and organizational preparedness.

Each engagement is scoped according to the client's environment and objectives, allowing our consultants to focus on the risks and security questions that matter most.
